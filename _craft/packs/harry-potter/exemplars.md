# Exemplars — harry-potter

Doctrine: `quiz-question.card.md` → `## Exemplars`. All five are diegetic, and the rule this
set is held to is that **the mechanic is visible in the stem** — a pressure a reader cannot
see is a pressure that is not there.

---

**1 — the portrait that likes you.** Pressure: portraits remember, and repeat it to whoever
asks. A camera has no preference; this one has picked a side, and that is the dilemma.

> A portrait in the corridor saw who did it, and it has started telling a kinder version than the one you gave.
> - Let the kinder version stand.
> - Correct it, in front of whoever is listening.
> - Ask it why it bothered.
> - Stay out of that corridor until term ends.

**2 — past the second buoy.** Pressure: a promise can be made binding. Swapped in 2026-08-28
for *one word of magic*, which turned on the Trace — a pressure now off this pack's list,
because it does not fire inside the castle and three of the five units built on it were set
there. This one also carries the **handback**: the last sentence is `Question.ask`, rendered
under the scene as a separate line. It is written inline here because the linter reads scene
and handback as one prompt.

> The boy you came down with is past the second buoy and not coming up, and the oath you swore on your wand was never to swim here alone. Nobody else on the bank.
> - In. Let the oath take what it takes.
> - Run for the boathouse pole.
> - Shout the castle down from where you stand.
> - Go for a teacher — four minutes there and back.

**3 — sworn, not said.** Pressure: a promise can be made binding. The cost is shown rather
than described — the boy who broke his is in the room, and what it took is visible.

> She wants it sworn, not said, and the boy who broke his last winter still cannot say his brother's name.
> - Swear it. You meant it anyway.
> - Say it plainly instead, and let her decide if that is enough.
> - Ask what she is afraid of, and swear to that alone.
> - Refuse, and stay to hear what she does about it.

**4 — the burnt hand.** Pressure: the ones who serve you are owned. The bondage is on the
page — it punishes itself for disobeying — so no ordinary employment version of this exists.

> The one who cleans the dormitory shut its own hand in the grate for lying to the caretaker about where you were.
> - Tell the caretaker the truth now.
> - Order it never to do that again.
> - Sit with it and put the fire out.
> - Take the punishment off it by taking the blame.

**5 — the second name.** Pressure: blood decides how a room treats you before you speak. The
register carries parentage and is read aloud, so the pause is the whole event.

> The register lists both your parents beside your name, and the prefect reading it out has stopped at the second one.
> - Say it for him.
> - Wait him out, however long it takes.
> - Ask him to start again from the top.
> - Answer to the first name only, and sit down.
